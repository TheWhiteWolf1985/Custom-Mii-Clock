# Support and issue reporting

[Italiano](SUPPORT.md) · **English**

> Installer 1.5.2 · firmware 0.1.20 (032) · launcher 0.26.5 / versionCode 58
>
> Current tester release candidate. X04G only.

Open a repository issue including:

- Xiaomi Mi Smart Clock X04G / `mico_x04g` and the observed problem.
- Firmware 0.1.20 (032), launcher 0.26.5/58 and installer 1.5.2, identifying the component involved.
- Linux distribution/version, architecture and installer step number where relevant.
- Workflow used: first firmware installation, APK update or recovery.
- Expected result, actual result and screenshots/redacted report.

Use **Open log folder** and read the report before sharing. Do not attach dumps, raw journals with private data, serials, MAC addresses, accounts, passwords, tokens or keys. Do not blindly reproduce a failed write: stop without rebooting and follow [Recovery](Documentazione/en/09-Recovery.md).

LX04/other models, third-party firmware, images not matching official releases and undocumented erase/format/bypass procedures are outside support. The project does not manage personal Home Assistant, Google or MQTT credentials.
